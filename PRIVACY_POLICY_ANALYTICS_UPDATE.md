# Privacy Policy Updates for Hotjar, Google Analytics & Meta Pixel

## Overview
Adding Hotjar, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel requires **substantial** privacy policy changes. These tools significantly increase data collection, use third-party cookies, and enable cross-site tracking. Users in the EU/California must be notified and given opt-out options.

---

## CRITICAL LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS

⚠️ **IMPORTANT**: Before implementing these changes:

1. **Cookie Consent Banner Required**: EU law (GDPR) and many other jurisdictions require explicit consent BEFORE loading these tracking scripts. You'll need a cookie consent banner.

2. **Do Not Track**: California law (CCPA) requires honoring Do Not Track signals.

3. **Data Processing Agreements**: You may need to sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with Google and Meta for GDPR compliance.

4. **Opt-Out Mechanisms**: Must provide clear opt-out methods for users who don't want to be tracked.

5. **Material Change Notification**: Since this materially changes privacy practices, you should email existing users about these changes.
